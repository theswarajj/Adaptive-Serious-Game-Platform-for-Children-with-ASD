    Let’s have a great day at school!


    Today’s Goal: Complete Your School Day

    Meet your teacher, complete your classroom activities, ask for help when needed, join your classmates, and finish the school day.

    1. Enter the school.
    2. Meet the teacher.
    3. Complete a classroom activity.
    4. Ask for help when needed.
    5. Interact with a classmate.
    6. Take part in a group task.
    7. Enjoy break time.
    8. Complete the school day.

    How to Play

    • Move with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
    • Drag the mouse (or finger) to turn the camera.
    • Press E or tap the button to interact.
    • Read the situation, then choose one of five answers.
    • Use Hint whenever you like. Retries are always allowed. Press 1–5 to choose an answer and Enter to continue.
    • Follow the orange arrow and the mini-map to find where to go next.
    • Follow the yellow objective and the glowing circle to complete your school day.
