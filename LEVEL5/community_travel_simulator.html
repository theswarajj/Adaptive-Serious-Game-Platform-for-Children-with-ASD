    Let’s take the bus and reach the library!


    Today’s Goal: Travel to the Library

    Visit the bus stop, take the correct bus, and get off at the library.

    1. Reach the bus stop.
    2. Wait for the bus.
    3. Board the bus.
    4. Confirm the destination.
    5. Travel to the library.
    6. Get off at the correct stop.

    How to Play

    • Move with W, A, S, D or the arrow keys.
    • Drag the mouse (or finger) to turn the camera.
    • Press E or tap the button to interact.
    • Read the situation, then choose one of five answers.
    • Use Hint whenever you like. Retries are always allowed.
    • Follow the yellow objective to reach the library.
